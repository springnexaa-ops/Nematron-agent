Nexa AI Trust Center

Nexa AI is designed as an account-based AI service. Public users have their own accounts; administrators have a separate private control plane. Access, safety, privacy and usage controls are enforced at the server, not only in the interface.

1. Privacy Policy

Nexa AI may process information that you submit to provide requested AI features, including prompts, uploaded documents or images, voice recordings sent for transcription, account information and service telemetry. We use such information to operate, secure, troubleshoot and improve the service as permitted by the applicable service configuration and law.

We do not ask users to submit secrets, passwords, API keys, financial credentials or unnecessary sensitive personal information. Do not enter information you are not authorized to share.

Authentication passwords are processed using salted PBKDF2 hashing and are not returned by the API. Session tokens are revocable. Public-user and administrator credentials are separate.

Current deployment notice: the user registry and sessions are presently held in Worker memory. This is suitable for controlled testing but is not a substitute for a durable production identity database. Before public launch, persistent encrypted storage, retention rules and account recovery infrastructure should be enabled.

2. Data Rights & Controls

Access

Authenticated users can view their account identity through the account endpoint.

Correction

Account details can be corrected through the supported account-management workflow.

Deletion

Account deletion should be requested through the service operator until the persistent identity store and self-service deletion workflow are enabled.

Security

Use a unique password and sign out on shared devices. Never share session tokens.

3. Acceptable Use — Do

  • Use Nexa AI for lawful, authorized and constructive purposes.
  • Verify important outputs before acting on them.
  • Use appropriate professional review for medical, legal, financial, employment and other high-impact decisions.
  • Protect other people's personal information and obtain appropriate permission before uploading it.
  • Report security vulnerabilities responsibly to the service operator.
  • Keep your account credentials private.

4. Acceptable Use — Do Not

  • Do not attempt to bypass authentication, quotas, safety controls or administrator approval.
  • Do not probe, scan, exploit or disrupt the infrastructure.
  • Do not use Nexa AI for credential theft, malware, fraud, harassment, impersonation or unauthorized surveillance.
  • Do not upload illegal material or material you do not have the right to process.
  • Do not use AI output as a substitute for qualified emergency or clinical care.
  • Do not automate abusive traffic, credential stuffing or denial-of-service activity.
  • Do not attempt to extract secrets, system prompts, private user data or administrator credentials.

5. AI Safety & Restrictions

Nexa AI follows a safety-oriented model architecture comparable in principle to modern consumer AI services: authentication and authorization at the gateway, rate limits, provider controls, abuse prevention, privacy boundaries and server-side enforcement.

AI output can be incorrect, incomplete, biased or outdated. The service should not be represented as a human professional or as an autonomous authority. Medical tools provide informational/decision-support assistance and do not replace a licensed clinician, emergency service or diagnostic responsibility.

Requests that create meaningful risk of harm, facilitate illegal activity, expose private information or attempt to defeat security controls may be refused, restricted or rate-limited.

6. Security Architecture

Authentication

Public login is separate from administrator login. New public accounts remain Pending until accepted by an administrator.

Password Protection

Passwords are salted and derived with PBKDF2-SHA-256; plaintext passwords are not stored.

Sessions

Short-lived bearer sessions are used, expired sessions are purged and administrator changes revoke affected user sessions.

Rate Limiting

Registration, login and AI endpoints have server-side limits to reduce abuse and credential attacks.

Admin Isolation

Administrative user-management APIs require the private administrator credential and are not available to public accounts.

Security Headers

Deployment uses anti-framing, MIME-sniffing, referrer, permissions and cross-origin security headers.

7. Account & Usage Model

Nexa AI uses a public-account model rather than exposing an administrator account to normal users. The service gateway identifies the authenticated user and applies per-user AI limits. Current baseline limits are server enforced and may change with service plans, capacity and abuse conditions.

Administrator tools are restricted to the private control plane. Public users cannot approve themselves, change their authorization status, access other users' records or call administrator endpoints.

8. Cookies, Tracking & Third Parties

The current public authentication implementation stores its bearer session in browser session storage and sends it over HTTPS to the Nexa AI service. Provider APIs may be used for optional AI, speech or vision functionality when configured. Data sent to an external provider is subject to that provider's applicable terms and privacy practices.

No claim is made here that every optional provider has identical retention or training practices. Production deployment should document each enabled provider and its data-processing terms before launch.

9. Changes & Contact

Policies, safety controls, usage limits and supported providers may change as Nexa AI evolves. The effective policy version should be reviewed before material use of the service.

For security reports or account issues, contact the SpringNexa service operator through its official support/contact channel.
