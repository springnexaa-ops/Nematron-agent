Nexa AI Architecture

Nexa AI uses a layered architecture rather than exposing a model directly to the public. The design separates identity, safety, orchestration, tools, model providers, data boundaries and administration.

01

Public Identity & Access

Users register through the public account portal. New accounts are Pending until administrator approval. Accepted accounts authenticate with a password-derived credential and a short-lived session. Administrator authentication is a separate control plane.

RegistrationLoginApprovalSession
02

API Gateway & Abuse Protection

Every protected AI request passes through server-side authentication and per-user rate limiting. Registration and login have additional anti-abuse limits. The gateway is the enforcement point, so client-side JavaScript cannot bypass account restrictions.

AuthZRate limitsAbuse controls
03

Safety & Policy Layer

Requests are evaluated against service safety requirements and high-risk use restrictions before provider execution. The system can refuse or restrict requests involving harmful activity, privacy violations, security bypasses or other prohibited use. Safety controls are designed to operate independently of the selected model.

SafetyPrivacyPolicy
04

Nexa Orchestrator

The orchestration layer selects the appropriate capability and provider. General chat, medical routing, voice, vision, document workflows and research functions are routed through controlled service endpoints rather than exposing provider credentials to browsers.

ChatMedicalVoiceVisionResearch
05

Model & Tool Gateway

Nexa AI can use Cloudflare Workers AI and optional external model providers. Provider secrets remain server-side. Provider selection, fallback behavior and model configuration are controlled by the Worker environment.

Model routingFallbacksSecrets isolation
06

Specialized Tools

Medical directory, image analysis, document processing, speech recognition, translation, text-to-speech and research workflows are exposed as bounded capabilities. Tools should validate input and return only the information necessary for the requested operation.

Least privilegeValidationTool isolation
07

Privacy & Data Boundary

Passwords and provider API secrets are never returned to clients. Public-user data is logically separated from administrator controls. Optional external providers are treated as separate data processors and should be enabled only with appropriate contractual and privacy review.

Data minimizationSecretsIsolation
08

Observability & Governance

Operational health and usage controls are separated from user-facing content. Administrator diagnostics are protected. Production deployments should use durable audit logging, persistent identity storage, retention policies, incident response and monitoring.

MonitoringAuditIncident response

Modern AI Service Principles

Like other major AI assistants, Nexa AI is intended to provide a conversational interface over a controlled backend rather than a raw model endpoint. The implementation is original and does not copy proprietary code, prompts or internal systems of ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or other providers.

Important: “same architecture” means comparable security and product principles—not access to or replication of another company's private infrastructure.
